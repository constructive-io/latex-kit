## LaTeX Kit ecosystem

Make contracts and other PDFs from typed LaTeX templates.

### 🚀 Starters

* [LaTeX Kit boilerplates](https://github.com/constructive-io/latex-kit-boilerplates): start a document project, workspace, or template package.
* [GitHub Action](https://github.com/constructive-io/latex-kit/tree/main/action): build and verify PDFs in CI.

### 📄 Templates

* [LaTeX Kit templates](https://github.com/constructive-io/latex-kit-templates): starting points for letters, invoices, services agreements, and NDAs. Templates are not legal advice.

### 📦 Key packages

* [latex-kit](https://www.npmjs.com/package/latex-kit): the CLI for scaffolding projects, checking records, and building PDFs.
* [@latex-kit/template](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@latex-kit/template): fill real LaTeX templates with typed data.
* [@latex-kit/components](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@latex-kit/components): compose tables, exhibits, and signature blocks.
* [@latex-kit/sign](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@latex-kit/sign): collect PDF signatures with a sealed audit trail.

[See all LaTeX Kit packages](https://github.com/constructive-io/latex-kit#packages).
